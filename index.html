Chapter 04 — The science of light

Light is more than what you see.

Light exposure at specific times can influence circadian rhythms — the internal timing system that helps regulate your sleep and wake cycle.

  1. 01 — The clock

    Inside you, a 24-hour rhythm.

    Your body runs on an internal clock that shapes when you feel alert and when you feel sleepy.

  2. 02 — The signal

    Light is its strongest cue.

    Light reaching the eyes is one of the main signals the brain uses to set that clock.

  3. 03 — The wavelength

    Blue-green, around 500 nm.

    retimer delivers light in the blue-green range — a part of the spectrum the circadian system responds to.

  4. 04 — The timing

    When matters as much as how.

    Morning light tends to shift the clock earlier. Light later in the day can shift it later.

  5. 05 — In short

    The right light, at the right time.

    Your best timing depends on your schedule and goals. The retimer app helps you plan it.
